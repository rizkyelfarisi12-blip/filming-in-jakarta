Jakarta Film Summit

Welcome to the Future of Global Co-Production

Where Global Productions Meet Real Execution. Bridging international vision with Indonesia's untapped cinematic potential.

The Jakarta Film Summit (JFS) is Asia’s premier B2B infrastructure and film supply chain conference.

Forget everything you know about traditional film festivals. Hosted by the newly established Jakarta Film Commission (JFC), this 3-day immersive event bridges global studios, OTT executives, and investors with Jakarta's elite, globally-certified production ecosystem.

We are not here just to celebrate cinema; we are here to build it. Say goodbye to bureaucratic red tape and discover how Jakarta’s new "Green-Lane" permitting system and cost-efficient infrastructure can maximize your next blockbuster's budget without compromising on quality.

The "Reverse-Pitch" Boardroom

Bring your upcoming script or project concept. In this highly exclusive VIP session, our consortium of top-tier, certified local vendors will pitch to you—providing a comprehensive execution blueprint and demonstrating the massive budget arbitrage you gain by shooting in Jakarta.

The Jakarta Lookbook & The VVIP Familiarization Tour (Fam-Tour)

Explore the unseen beauty of Jakarta before you even step outside. Experience our catalog featuring iconic Neo-Metropolis skylines, gritty historical heritage sites, and private tropical islands—complete with logistical data ready for your technical scout and Join our exclusive ground tour to experience Jakarta’s diverse cinematic landscapes firsthand. From underground transit hubs to a sunset cruise in our pristine archipelago, witness the readiness of our infrastructure.

The JFC Certified Partner (JCP) Vendor Expo

Connect directly with Jakarta’s finest. Access a curated marketplace of world-class camera rentals, logistics providers, and post-production studios—all rigorously vetted and certified by the government to meet international safety and compliance standards.
