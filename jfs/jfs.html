Jakarta Film Summit is a strategic platform designed for professional high-impact dialogue.

We go beyond the traditional film festival model to focus exclusively on the mechanics of execution. The Summit serves as the nexus for global producers, government stakeholders, and local industry leaders to forge lasting partnerships.

Our mission is to streamline the pathway from creative concept to physical production, ensuring that Indonesia's diverse landscapes and skilled workforce are accessible to the world's most ambitious storytellers.
